# Gustav social banner R3 — exact copy decision

## Recommended exact banner copy package

Use the same copy hierarchy for LinkedIn and X.

- **Headline:** `Skab mere frihed i din virksomhed.`
- **Optional support:** `Find flaskehalsen. Gør kun det, der skal til.`
- **Context:** `Offer · Operations · Demand`
- **Brand rail:** `Gustav Online → onlinesourdough → Arc'IT AI`

Character counts, including spaces and punctuation, are 34 for the headline,
45 for the support line, 27 for the context line, and 43 for the brand rail.

The headline, context, and ordered brand rail are the core package. The support
line is secondary and may be removed as one complete line when a responsive crop
cannot keep it comfortably readable. Do not shorten it word by word or replace
it with the R2 wording.

## Why this is the strongest decision

`Skab mere frihed i din virksomhed.` keeps the strategic core the owner already
likes and states Business Freedom as a direction rather than an absolute end
state. `Mere` matters: it makes room for gradual gains in control over time,
capacity/involvement, and direction, and therefore stays honest without
weakening the line. The sentence is natural Danish and remains short enough to
carry the banner at a glance.

The optional support line translates the method into ordinary Danish.
`Flaskehalsen` carries the meaning of the current constraint without the mixed
Danish/English phrasing, while `Gør kun det, der skal til` preserves the
smallest-useful-change principle without the awkward adjective `nyttige`. It
does not promise a measured outcome or imply a total transformation.

`Offer · Operations · Demand` remains unchanged because it is the shortest
source-grounded expression of the holistic operating frame. Together with the
brand rail, it preserves **person → method → delivery**: Gustav Online is the
personal/public entry, onlinesourdough is the method and offer layer, and
Arc'IT AI is the delivery layer.

## Material alternatives considered

### `Skab frihed i din virksomhed.`

This is five characters shorter and slightly harder in rhythm. It was not
selected because dropping `mere` turns an incremental direction into a more
absolute-sounding promise. The source material supports creating more control
and freedom, not a binary state called freedom. The small space saving is not
worth the loss of honesty and nuance.

### No support line

This is the correct responsive fallback and gives the strongest large-scale
readability. It was not chosen as the default because the method line adds
useful differentiation: freedom is pursued by finding the current bottleneck
and limiting the intervention, not by making a broad transformation claim.

### `Find dit nuværende constraint. Gør den mindste nyttige ændring.`

This R2 line is source-faithful but rejected for the final banner. `Dit
nuværende constraint` reads like internal method terminology, and `den mindste
nyttige ændring` feels translated rather than spoken. It is also 18 characters
longer than the selected support line.

### `Mere kontrol over tid, kapacitet og retning.`

This is precise supporting language for Business Freedom, but it is more
consultative and less memorable as a headline. It explains the concept instead
of giving the banner one clear invitation.

## ADS layout direction

Remove all secondary role labels below the three brand names. Do not show
`Content & Demand`, `Method & Offers`, `DFY Delivery`, or shortened substitutes
such as `Content · Method · Delivery`. Their meaning is already carried by the
context line and the fixed reading order, so at banner scale the labels add
density rather than necessary understanding.

Keep the three real brand names or marks in exactly this semantic order:

`Gustav Online → onlinesourdough → Arc'IT AI`

Use one shared baseline and a restrained directional connector or progression
to make the relationship legible as person → method → delivery. The three
brands must not look like unrelated sponsors or equal product tiles. Do not
invent taglines, merge their identities, or make the arrows more prominent
than the names.

Hierarchy and fallback order:

1. Headline — one line and visually dominant.
2. Context — one line and clearly secondary.
3. Brand rail — one coherent line or grouped sequence, with all three names.
4. Optional support — one line when readable; remove it first under crop or
   scale pressure.

Keep necessary copy and all three brands away from the lower-left profile-image
overlap. Test the LinkedIn 1584×396 and X 1500×500 compositions at their likely
mobile crops. Extra height on X should create space, not additional copy.

Source precedence for this decision is:

1. Current owner feedback in the R3 worker contract.
2. The accepted synthesis and source-to-claim map in the [R2 positioning
   decision](../gustav-social-banner-r2/POSITIONING.md).
3. The accepted business sources cited by R2, especially *Freedom-first
   Business Model*, *The onlinesourdough Method*, *onlinesourdough — Offers*,
   *Personal brand / Content Eco System*, and *Work with onlinesourdough*.

This package introduces no outcome guarantee, metric, customer proof, new offer
architecture, autonomy claim, or claim that the business can operate without
its owner. It changes only the editorial expression and banner hierarchy. It
does not alter AIOS, ADS, Notion, any profile, or any external service, and it
does not authorize publishing.
